GRASS LINE

Start with one farm address.

Search a U.S. farm, town, or county. Farmer Helper geocodes the place and builds the report around its exact coordinates.

Workflow: Address → location → field report.

TOPSOIL

Read the soil your roots use.

USDA Soil Data Access supplies soil series, pH, texture, drainage, organic matter, and water-holding context for the location.

Data source: USDA soil survey, with SoilGrids as a fallback.

SUBSOIL

Track moisture and recent weather.

NASA observations summarize recent rainfall, topsoil moisture, and the conditions that affect access, planting, and irrigation decisions.

Data sources: NASA POWER and NASA soil-moisture data.

PARENT MATERIAL

Plan around the growing season.

Long-term climate averages drive planting windows and crop recommendations, with reasons tied to temperature, water, and soil fit.

Key takeaway: Crop rules turn climate into a next step.

BEDROCK

Bring the local picture together.

Finish with one report: maps, satellite layers, crop timing, extension guidance, nearby farm resources, and current agricultural news.

The result: The Field Desk connects data to action.
